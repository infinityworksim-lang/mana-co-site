The MANA Healthcare Platform

One platform for connected healthcare workflows.

MedicalSpace is the primary healthcare platform developed by MANA LLC. It brings together practical tools for doctors and healthcare professionals across patient management, clinical workflows, professional collaboration, communication, medical information, and imaging.

  • Patient and medical record management
  • AI-assisted clinical workflows
  • Visits, prescriptions, and documentation
  • Doctor-to-doctor collaboration
  • Medical posts and clinical cases
  • Voice, audio, and video communication
  • Medical documents and DICOM images
  • Speech-to-text and multilingual tools
