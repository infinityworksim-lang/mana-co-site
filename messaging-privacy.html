Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the MANA Messaging System processes information when healthcare facilities send medical reports to patients through WhatsApp.

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

About the MANA Messaging System

MANA LLC provides the MANA Messaging System, a service that enables healthcare facilities to send medical reports to patients through WhatsApp.

1. Information We Process

The service may process patients’ phone numbers, medical reports, and message delivery information as necessary to provide the messaging service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to facilitate the delivery of medical reports to intended recipients through WhatsApp on behalf of the healthcare facility initiating the message.

3. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by Meta/WhatsApp and service providers as necessary to operate the service and deliver messages. MANA LLC does not sell patients’ personal information.

4. Data Retention

Medical reports are temporarily stored for delivery purposes and are scheduled for automatic deletion the following day.

5. Privacy Responsibilities

Healthcare facilities are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authorization to send patients’ medical information through the service.

6. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the MANA Messaging System, please contact MANA LLC.
