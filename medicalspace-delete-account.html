Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how MedicalSpace users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your MedicalSpace Account

MedicalSpace provides account deletion options for users who wish to stop using the service and request deletion of their account and associated data.

1

Open MedicalSpace

Sign in to your MedicalSpace account using your registered account credentials.

2

Open Account Settings

Navigate to the account or profile settings available in the MedicalSpace application.

3

Request Account Deletion

Select the account deletion option and follow the confirmation steps provided by the application.

Can't access your account? You can contact MANA LLC using the email address below to request account and data deletion.

Account deletion requests:

info@mana-co.com

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the user's account, subscription status, and information associated with the account, deletion may include data such as:

Account Information

Account and profile information associated with the MedicalSpace user account.

User-Associated Data

Data associated with the user's MedicalSpace account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

User Content

Eligible content and communications associated with the account may be deleted in accordance with applicable policies.

Account Settings

Account preferences and other information associated with the user's account may be removed.

Data Retention

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period after an account deletion request when required for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, financial, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Where retention is required, the information will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose and handled according to MANA LLC's privacy and data-management practices.

For additional information about how MedicalSpace collects, uses, stores, and protects information, please review the MedicalSpace Privacy Policy.

View Privacy Policy

Deletion Request Processing

When a deletion request is submitted, MANA LLC may need to verify the requester's identity and account ownership before processing the request.

This verification helps protect MedicalSpace accounts and prevents unauthorized deletion requests.

Additional information may be requested when reasonably necessary to verify ownership of the account or to comply with applicable legal requirements.
