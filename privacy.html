1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through:

  • the MedicalSpace mobile application;
  • MedicalSpace websites and web services;
  • MedicalSpace APIs and backend services;
  • communications and support services;
  • subscription and billing services;
  • AI-assisted features;
  • voice, transcription, translation, messaging, and communication features; and
  • other services operated by MANA in connection with MedicalSpace.

This Privacy Policy does not necessarily apply to third-party services that have their own privacy policies.

2. Important Healthcare Data Notice

MedicalSpace may be used by healthcare professionals to store and process information relating to their patients, including medical records and other health-related information.

The healthcare professional, clinic, organization, or other MedicalSpace account owner who enters or controls patient information is primarily responsible for determining the lawful basis for collecting, using, disclosing, and otherwise processing that patient information.

MANA provides the technology infrastructure used to store and process such information on behalf of the applicable MedicalSpace customer.

Depending on the applicable law and the specific processing activity, MANA may act as a service provider, processor, data processor, controller, or another legally recognized role.

Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to determine the legal classification of MANA or any MedicalSpace customer under a particular jurisdiction where the law requires a different classification.

3. Information We Collect

We may collect or receive the following categories of information.

3.1 Account Information

When a user creates or manages an account, we may collect:

  • full name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • professional information;
  • professional specialty;

  • country and city;
  • profile photograph;
  • camera-captured images;
  • clinic or organization information;
  • account credentials and authentication information;
  • language preferences;
  • account role;
  • subscription information; and
  • information necessary to administer the account.

3.2 Professional Information

Healthcare professionals may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • specialty;
  • years of experience;
  • professional biography;
  • clinic information;
  • professional profile information;
  • professional services;
  • professional contact information;
  • profile images and logos; and
  • information displayed to other authorized MedicalSpace users.

Users are responsible for ensuring that professional information they publish is accurate and lawful.

3.3 Patient Information

MedicalSpace may allow healthcare professionals to create and manage patient records.

Such information may include:

  • patient name;
  • age or date of birth;
  • telephone number;
  • demographic information;
  • medical history;
  • diagnoses;
  • symptoms;
  • complaints;
  • vital signs;
  • medications;
  • prescriptions;
  • treatment plans;
  • clinical notes;
  • visits;
  • medical documents;
  • laboratory results;
  • radiology and imaging information;
  • DICOM files;
  • photographs;
  • voice recordings;
  • transcriptions;
  • follow-up information; and
  • other health-related information entered by an authorized healthcare professional.

MANA does not independently determine the medical purposes for which a healthcare professional collects patient information.

The healthcare professional or organization using MedicalSpace is responsible for ensuring that it has the necessary legal authority, consent, authorization, notice, or other lawful basis required to process patient information.

4. User-Generated Content and Medical Records

Medical records, documents, notes, prescriptions, photographs, recordings, files, messages, and other information uploaded or entered by a MedicalSpace customer remain under the control of that customer, subject to applicable law and the contractual rights necessary for MANA to operate MedicalSpace.

MANA does not claim ownership of a customer's medical records merely because those records are stored using MedicalSpace.

MANA processes customer content only as necessary to provide, operate, secure, maintain, troubleshoot, synchronize, back up, and support MedicalSpace and its requested features, and as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

MANA does not sell customer medical records or personal health information.

MANA does not use customer medical records or personal health information to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where information is submitted to an AI-enabled feature, it may be processed by MANA and applicable third-party technology providers only as necessary to provide the requested AI functionality, subject to applicable contractual, technical, and legal safeguards.

5. Communications and Messages

MedicalSpace may process information contained in:

  • direct messages;
  • chat communications;
  • voice messages;
  • translated messages;
  • notifications;
  • support requests; and
  • other communications transmitted through the Platform.

Messages and communications may be stored to provide synchronization, delivery, security, auditing, and other Service functionality.

Users should not use MedicalSpace to transmit information that they are not legally authorized to transmit.

6. Voice, Microphone, and Audio Data

Certain MedicalSpace features may allow users to record, upload, or transmit voice or audio information.

MedicalSpace may access the device microphone when the user chooses to use features such as:

  • voice messages;
  • voice-based AI features;
  • speech-to-text transcription;
  • creation of visit notes;
  • audio or video communication;
  • voice summaries; or
  • other features that require audio input.

Depending on the feature, audio may be processed for purposes including:

  • speech-to-text transcription;
  • creation of visit notes;
  • AI-assisted clinical analysis;
  • voice summaries;
  • text-to-speech generation;
  • communication;
  • synchronization; and
  • other functionality explicitly requested by the user.

Audio and voice information is processed only as necessary to provide the feature requested by the user and to operate, secure, maintain, and support MedicalSpace.

Users are responsible for obtaining any legally required consent before recording or processing another person's voice.

MANA does not use customer voice recordings or transcriptions to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

MANA does not guarantee that any particular voice recording, transcription, translation, or AI-generated result will be error-free.

6A. Camera and Images

MedicalSpace may access the device camera when the user chooses to capture or update a profile photograph or when a MedicalSpace feature requires image capture.

Images captured or uploaded by the user may be stored and processed as necessary to provide the requested functionality.

MedicalSpace may also allow users to upload photographs, medical images, documents, or other visual information as part of patient management, medical records, communication, or other Service features.

Camera access is not used to continuously monitor users or to collect images when the user has not initiated a feature that requires camera access.

MANA does not use customer photographs, medical images, or medical records to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

7. Artificial Intelligence

MedicalSpace may provide features that use artificial intelligence and machine-learning technologies.

AI-assisted functionality may be used to:

  • summarize information;
  • transcribe voice recordings;
  • organize clinical information;
  • analyze information supplied by the user;
  • generate suggested clinical summaries;
  • identify potentially relevant information;
  • review prescriptions or treatment information;
  • generate voice summaries;
  • assist with documentation; and
  • provide other AI-assisted functionality explicitly requested by the user.

When a user submits information to an AI-enabled feature, that information may be processed by MANA and by third-party technology providers used to provide the requested AI functionality.

MANA uses appropriate contractual and technical measures designed to limit such processing to the purposes necessary to provide the requested functionality and to comply with applicable law.

MANA does not use customer medical records, personal health information, voice recordings, or other confidential customer content submitted to MedicalSpace to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

MANA may change or replace an underlying AI technology provider from time to time. Where required by applicable law, MANA will provide appropriate notice regarding material changes in how personal information is processed.

AI-Generated Information

AI-generated information:

  • is not medical advice;
  • is not a diagnosis;
  • is not a prescription;
  • is not a substitute for professional medical judgment;
  • is not a substitute for clinical examination;
  • is not guaranteed to be accurate, complete, current, or appropriate;
  • must be independently reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional before being relied upon; and
  • must not be used as the sole basis for any medical decision.

Users remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated information and for all clinical decisions and actions taken based on information generated through MedicalSpace.

8. DICOM and Medical Imaging

MedicalSpace may allow users to upload, view, store, or process medical imaging files, including DICOM files.

MANA provides technical functionality and does not independently interpret, diagnose, or validate medical images.

Any image interpretation, diagnosis, or clinical decision remains the responsibility of the appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

22. Data Retention and Deletion

MANA retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

When an individual user requests deletion of their MedicalSpace account, MANA will delete the personal information associated with that user account, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other lawful retention requirements.

Where MedicalSpace is used by a clinic, organization, or other account owner to manage patient records, patient records may be subject to separate medical-record retention requirements. Deletion of an individual user's MedicalSpace account does not automatically require deletion of patient records that are lawfully maintained by the applicable healthcare provider or organization.

Where applicable, MANA may retain limited information necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect the security of MedicalSpace, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or enforce legal rights.

Information retained for these purposes will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when the applicable retention period ends.

Information deleted from active systems may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until those backups are overwritten or securely deleted in accordance with MANA's backup and disaster-recovery procedures.

23. Account Termination and Patient Data

Users may request deletion of their MedicalSpace account through the account and data deletion process made available by MANA.

When an account deletion request is received, MANA will process the request within a reasonably prompt period and will delete the personal information associated with the applicable user account, subject to the retention exceptions described in Section 22.

Where patient records are maintained by a healthcare professional, clinic, or organization, those records may be subject to legal, regulatory, medical-record retention, or contractual requirements that prevent immediate deletion.

The healthcare professional, clinic, or organization responsible for the patient records remains responsible for determining the applicable retention requirements for those records.

MANA does not guarantee indefinite availability of information after account deletion or termination.

Users are responsible for exporting or otherwise obtaining records they are legally required to retain before requesting account deletion or terminating their account.
