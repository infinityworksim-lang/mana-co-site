Meet MedicalSpace

A healthcare technology platform designed for doctors and healthcare professionals to manage patients, medical information, communication, imaging, and AI-assisted workflows from one connected environment.

Explore MedicalSpace Contact MANA

One Platform for Modern Healthcare

MedicalSpace brings essential healthcare workflows together in a single platform designed for medical professionals.

Doctors can manage patient information, clinical documentation, communication, medical content, imaging, and AI-assisted workflows within a connected healthcare environment.

MedicalSpace is primarily designed for healthcare professionals and authorized medical-industry users.

MedicalSpace Features

A growing set of tools designed to support healthcare professionals throughout their daily workflows.

Patient Management

Organize patient information, visits, clinical notes, prescriptions, follow-ups, and other healthcare information in one place.

Medical Records

Manage clinical documentation, medical reports, laboratory information, prescriptions, and other healthcare records.

DICOM & Medical Imaging

Work with medical imaging and DICOM content as part of supported healthcare workflows.

Professional Communication

Communicate with other healthcare professionals through messaging and supported communication features.

Medical Professional Network

Connect with other doctors, share professional medical content, and participate in a healthcare professional community.

Voice & Speech

Use supported voice features including voice messages, speech-to-text, and AI voice interaction to support documentation and communication.

Medical Translation

Translate supported medical content and communications across multiple languages.

Audio & Video Communication

Support healthcare communication through available audio and video communication features.

AI-Assisted Healthcare Workflows

MedicalSpace includes AI-powered functionality designed to assist healthcare professionals with information processing, documentation, communication, and clinical workflows.

  • AI-assisted medical information and clinical workflows
  • Voice-based AI interaction
  • Medical text processing and translation
  • Assistance with clinical documentation
  • Support for healthcare information workflows

AI-generated information is intended to assist qualified healthcare professionals and does not replace professional medical judgment, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision-making.
